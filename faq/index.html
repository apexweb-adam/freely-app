Freely FAQ

Short, exact answers about Freely: Bible Scroll. If your question is not here, email info@apexweb.hu.

Is Freely free?

Yes. Freely is free to download, and the daily feed up to your goal (up to 20 passages a day), the whole Bible, listening, saving, sharing, streaks and the daily reminder are free for everyone, permanently. Freely Plus is optional.

What does Freely Plus cost?

In the US, Freely Plus is $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, $3.99 a week, or $59.99 once for lifetime access. Prices in other countries are shown in the app before you buy.

What does Freely Plus add?

An unlimited feed (keep scrolling after your daily goal, and set a goal of up to 50 passages), memorize mode, and all 12 card looks for the feed and for shared cards. Free readers get 4 card looks.

Does Freely show ads?

No. Freely shows no ads to anyone, free or paid, and contains no advertising or tracking code.

Which Bible translation does Freely use?

The Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a modern English translation dedicated to the public domain. It is the only translation in the app. No verse is written or changed by AI.

Can I read the whole Bible, not just the feed?

Yes. The Bible tab has every book and chapter of the BSB with no daily limit. You can also open the full chapter from any passage in the feed, with the passage highlighted.

Can Freely read the Bible aloud?

Yes. Tap Listen on any passage or the speaker button in any chapter. Freely uses the speech voice built into iOS, so it works offline. It is a synthesized voice, not a recorded audio Bible.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign up or sign in. Everything you save stays on your iPhone.

What data does Freely collect?

None. Saved passages, streaks, goals and settings are stored only on your device. There are no analytics and no tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple, and the app only checks with Apple whether Plus is active.

Do my saved verses sync to my iPad or a new phone?

No. Because Freely has no account and stores nothing on a server, saved passages and streaks live only on the iPhone where you created them. Deleting the app deletes them. Freely Plus can be restored on another device with the same Apple Account.

How does memorize mode work?

Open a passage in memorize mode and tap Hide more words. Each round hides more of the passage: a quarter, half, three quarters, then every word. Say it aloud and tap a blank to reveal a word when you get stuck. Memorize mode is part of Freely Plus.

How many passages are in the feed?

More than 560 hand picked passages across 18 topics, such as peace over anxiety, hope, strength, prayer, comfort in grief and forgiveness. About three in four come from the topics you choose.

Is Freely available on Android or iPad?

Freely is an iPhone app and needs iOS 17 or later. There is no Android version.

How do I cancel or restore Freely Plus?

To cancel, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Freely, at least a day before renewal. To restore, open Freely, go to Me, open Freely Plus and tap Restore with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Who makes Freely?

Freely is made by Adam Tokar, an independent developer. Contact: info@apexweb.hu.
