Freely privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Freely is a Bible reading app for iPhone. It is operated by Tokár Ádám, sole proprietor, Hungary ("we").

The short version

Freely collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your saved verses, reading place, streak, daily goal, chosen topics and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Read-aloud uses the speech voices built into iOS on your device.
  • The daily verse reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • When you save a verse card to Photos, Freely asks for permission to add images. It cannot read your photo library.

Purchases

Freely Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether Plus is active.

Children

Freely does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
