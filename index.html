Scroll the Word, not the feed.

Freely is an iPhone app that turns the Bible into a vertical feed. You swipe through short passages, one at a time, the way you would swipe through any other feed. The difference is what you are reading.

Download on the App Store

What you get for free

  • A daily feed of hand picked passages from across the Bible, grouped by the topics you choose, such as peace over anxiety, hope, strength, prayer or forgiveness.
  • A daily goal that you set yourself, 10 passages by default and up to 20 a day on the free plan, with a streak counter.
  • The whole Berean Standard Bible, every book and chapter, with no limit.
  • Listening: any passage or chapter can be read aloud by your iPhone.
  • Saving and sharing passages as verse cards.
  • No account, no ads and no tracking.

Freely Plus

Plus removes the daily limit in the feed, adds memorize mode and unlocks all 12 card looks. It costs $29.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, $3.99 a week, or $59.99 once for lifetime access (US prices). Everything in the free list above stays free.
