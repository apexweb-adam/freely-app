About Freely

Freely: Bible Scroll is an iPhone app for people who want to spend some of the time they lose to social media feeds in Scripture instead. It shows the Bible as a vertical feed: one passage fills the screen, you read it, you swipe up for the next one.

Get Freely on the App Store

Who it is for

Freely is for Christians, and for anyone curious about the Bible, who already pick up their phone many times a day and would like some of those minutes to go to something that lasts. It suits you if:

  • you have tried reading plans and fallen behind when a chapter a day felt like too much;
  • you open your phone out of habit and want that habit to lead somewhere better;
  • you want verses on a specific need, such as anxiety, grief, courage or forgiveness, without having to search for them;
  • you want to learn passages by heart in small steps.

Freely is not a full Bible study suite. It has no commentaries, no original language tools, no notes and no reading groups. If you need those, the Bible app comparison names the apps that do them well.

What it does

The feed

The feed draws on more than 560 hand picked passages from across the whole Bible, from the Psalms and Proverbs to the Gospels and the letters, grouped into 18 topics: peace over anxiety, hope, strength, God's love, faith, wisdom, self-control, purpose, prayer, comfort in grief, forgiveness, who I am in Christ, relationships, work and money, gratitude, courage, words about Jesus, and praise. You choose your topics when you start and can change them at any time. About three in four passages come from your topics and the rest from the others, so you still meet the wider Bible. Passages you have already read do not come back until you have seen the rest.

Every card in the feed has buttons to listen, save, share, memorize, and open the full chapter. Opening the chapter shows the passage highlighted in its context, so a verse is never read in isolation if you want to see what surrounds it. Double tap a card to save it.

A daily goal and a streak

You set a daily goal of passages, from 3 to 20 on the free plan (10 by default). A ring shows your progress, the app celebrates when you reach the goal, and a streak counter shows how many days in a row you have read. The Me tab shows your current and best streak and the last seven days.

The whole Bible

The Bible tab contains every book and chapter of the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a modern English translation that its translators have dedicated to the public domain. There is no daily limit on the Bible tab. You can find a book by name, continue where you stopped, and change the text size.

Listening

Any passage in the feed and any chapter in the Bible tab can be read aloud. Freely uses the speech voice built into iOS on your iPhone, so it works without an internet connection and nothing is sent anywhere. It is a synthesized voice, not a recorded narrator.

Saving and sharing

Saved passages collect in the Saved tab. Any passage can be shared as an image card or saved to your Photos. There are 12 card looks (Night, Dawn, Olive, Parchment, Galilee, Ember, Lilac, Wheat, Cedar, Sharon, Stone and Snow); 4 are free and all 12 come with Plus.

Memorize mode (Plus)

Memorize mode takes any passage and hides more of its words each round: none, then a quarter, half, three quarters, and finally all of them. You say the passage aloud and tap a blank when you get stuck. The memorization guide explains why this method works.

One daily reminder

If you turn it on, Freely sends one notification a day with a verse, at the time you choose. Nothing else.

Pricing, in plain numbers

How privacy works

Freely collects no personal data. There is no account and no sign in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your saved passages, streak, goal, topics and settings are stored only on your iPhone, and deleting the app deletes them. Read aloud uses the voices on your device. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. When you save a card to Photos, Freely asks only for permission to add images; it cannot see your library. Purchases go through Apple, so the app never sees your name or payment details. The full text is in the privacy policy.

One consequence of this design: because there is no account, your saved passages and streak do not sync between devices.

Where the text comes from

All Scripture in Freely is the Berean Standard Bible, unchanged. No verse in Freely is written, paraphrased or altered by AI. The passages in the feed were chosen by hand and grouped by topic.
