Freely support

Questions, a wrong verse, or a problem with Freely Plus? Email info@apexweb.hu. A screenshot helps.

Restore Freely Plus

Open Freely, go to Me, open Freely Plus and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel a subscription

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Freely. Cancel at least a day before it renews.
